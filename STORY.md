# The Last World — Story Bible (v1.0)

> Tone: epic and melancholic. A magnificent world slowly going dark, characters who
> have already lost something, and a mystery at the heart of the hero.

---

## 1. The world

### The Great Pallor
There were once thousands of worlds, joined like the branches of a single tree:
the **Sky Tree**. Five centuries ago, a nameless force — the **Pallor** — began erasing
them one by one. No war, no fire: the colors dull, the sounds die out, memories are
forgotten, and then nothing is left.

**Aerendal** is the last world. The last living shoot of the Tree.

### The Celestial Seal
To save Aerendal, the four **First Guardians** (one per elemental wind) forged the
**Celestial Seal**: an invisible dome that holds the Pallor at a distance. To power it,
they bound their own souls to it. The people of Aerendal worship them as sleeping gods;
they do not know the Guardians are suffering, imprisoned in the Seal for five hundred
years.

### The four winds
The Seal channels the **four winds** that keep the world alive:
| Wind | Element | Region (chapter) | What it carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clear Wind | Anemo | Clearwind Meadows (Ch. I) | breath, motion |
| Ember Wind | Pyro | Cendrelune Heights (Ch. II) | warmth, will |
| Tide Wind | Hydro | Ysmère Lagoon (Ch. III) | memory, dreams |
| Storm Wind | Electro | Valdrak Peaks (Ch. IV) | lightning, fate |

When the Seal shattered (a month before the start of the game), it burst into **four
fragments**. The winds fell silent. Without them, the Pallor is already gnawing at the
edges of the island: on the horizon, the sea is losing its color and the sky is
"fading" (a recurring visual motif).

## 2. The characters

### Aelis — the hero (Anemo)
Fell from the sky at the foot of the Altar of Winds, with no memory at all. The wind
obeys him by instinct. Calm, curious, with a slightly clumsy kindness.
**Secret (revealed gradually):** Aelis is the soul of **Aelith**, the First Guardian of
the Clear Wind, freed from the Seal when it shattered. The body he inhabits was shaped
by the wind itself. His memories remained in the fragments.

### Kael — the double (tragic antagonist)
"A traveler who looked exactly like you." He is the one who shattered the Seal. Kael is
Aelith's **twin brother**, five centuries ago. He refused to sacrifice himself, survived
alone aging outside of time, and spent five hundred years looking for a way to free his
sister. He ended up breaking the Seal knowing it might doom Aerendal. He is not cruel:
he is tired, and he loves. He wants to stop Aelis from restoring the Seal, because doing
so would seal them inside it forever.
*Key line: "Does a world that survives only by chaining its gods deserve to be saved?"*

### Elara — the Keeper of the Altar
Priestess of the Altar of Winds, 40 years old, gentle but inflexible. She has spent her
life praying to Guardians she has never seen. She watched Kael shatter the Seal and could
do nothing: that is her guilt. She serves as Aelis's guide and surrogate mother.

### Kaira — the ember swordswoman (Pyro)
A 22-year-old mercenary from the Cendrelune Heights, after the Pallor swallowed her home
village. Headstrong, funny, refuses to cry. She fights because standing still terrifies
her. Running thread: she is searching for her little brother, **Ilio**, lost to the Pallor.

### Nerys — the scholar of tides (Hydro)
26-year-old archivist from Ysmère, who came to study the Sentinels (teleportation
obelisks, linked by underground water currents). Passionate, absent-minded, she talks too
much when she is afraid. She suspects the truth about the Guardians before anyone else.

### Torvan — the old warden (Electro)
A 61-year-old soldier who has guarded the ancient ruins for thirty years, out of an oath
to a vanished order (the **Order of the Vigil**). Gruff, protective, smokes a pipe. He
knows part of the truth about the Seal and has kept silent his whole life.

### Bram — the merchant
The camp's jovial merchant, a former adventurer. Comic relief, but he has seen the
Pallor up close and carries a faded scar on his arm from it.

### The Ancient Guardian (Ch. I boss)
A colossus of stone and wind, created by the First Guardians to protect the Tower of
Winds. It no longer recognizes anyone. When it falls, it murmurs: *"...Aelith? You
came back..."* — the first clue to the hero's identity.

## 4. Version 0.1 — Prologue and Chapter I in detail

### Prologue — "The fall" (cutscene CIN_00)
Black screen. A woman's voice (Aelith, not yet recognizable):
> "There were once a thousand worlds. They went out like candles, without a sound.
> Only one remains."

Wide shot of Aerendal from the sky at sunset; on the horizon the sky is pale, washed
out. A shooting star crosses the clouds: it is Aelis falling.
> "If you can hear my voice… then the Seal is broken. Forgive me. Wake up."

Aelis lands softly at the foot of the Altar, carried by a whirlwind of wind. Fade to white.

### Act 1 — The awakening
- **CIN_01 "Awakening"**: Aelis opens his eyes. Elara, leaning over, looks at him. The
  camera reveals the Clearwind Meadows. Then dialogue with Elara (already exists, rewritten).
- Objective: touch the Altar.
- **CIN_02 "Resonance"**: Aelis places his hand on the Altar. Flash: a vision of a figure
  identical to Aelis (Kael), standing in the storm, shattering the Seal with one sword
  strike. The four shards fly off in four directions. Aelis comes back to himself,
  breathless.
  > Elara: "You saw it too, didn't you… He had your face."

### Act 2 — The companions
- **Kaira**: arrives mid-fight against slimes near the camp ("Are you going to stand
  there watching all day?"). She joins the party after the fight.
- **Nerys**: met near a Sentinel, measuring it. After three Sentinels are activated, she
  realizes Aelis "resonates" with them and follows him.
- **Torvan**: guards the ruins. Distrustful at first, he points his weapon at Aelis,
  mistaking him for Kael, then catches himself. After the chest, he hands over the
  fragment and joins the party "to keep an eye on you".

### Act 3 — The Tower of Winds
- Elara reads the fragment's runes: the key is at the top of the Tower of Winds.
- Climb up the tower, ancient chest.
- **CIN_03 "The Ancient Guardian"**: the ground trembles, the arena lights up, the
  colossus rises, turquoise eyes. Title on screen: *Ancient Guardian — Sentinel of the Tower*.
- Boss fight.
- **CIN_04 "The first wind"**: the Guardian collapses ("...Aelith? You came back...").
  The fragment rises, the Clear Wind is freed: grass ripples as far as the eye can see,
  the clouds move again, color returns to the nearest horizon.
  Final shot: on a distant cliff, a hooded figure (Kael) watches, then vanishes in a gust.

### Chapter I epilogue (CIN_05 "He looked like you")
Back with Elara. Dialogue about the three remaining fragments. Then a night shot:
Kael alone at the edge of the world, facing the Pallor.
> Kael: "You were always stubborn, little sister. This time, I will not let you
> sacrifice yourself."
Title card: *Chapter II — The Ashes of Cendrelune (coming soon)*.

## 5. Writing rules
- Short lines of dialogue (2 lines max per bubble), lightly elevated register,
  characters use informal address with each other; Torvan alone addresses Elara formally.
- Aelis speaks little: he asks questions, and the player discovers the story along with him.
- Every NPC has a tic: Kaira ("Come on!"), Nerys (learned digressions), Torvan
  ("Hmpf."), Bram (talks about prices).
- Tutorials are slipped into Elara's lines, never a raw text screen.
- Aelis's gender: male in the current text (the model is male). Kael calls him "little
  sister" only when speaking about Aelith's soul: this is intentional, it keeps the
  mystery alive.

## 6. Chapters II to X (v0.2 → v1.0)

The four First Guardians: **Aelith** (Anemo), **Cendrel** (Pyro), **Ysmé** (Hydro), **Valdrak** (Electro).
Each chapter starts with a briefing from Elara at the camp and ends with a cutscene; quests live in
`ChapterQuests.cs`, text in `ChapterDialogue.cs`, locations in `ChapterBuilder*.cs`, scenes in `ChapterCinematics.cs`.

### Ch. II — The Ashes of Cendrelune (Pyro · Kaira)
A burned village, Maren (its last inhabitant), the Volcano Forge (sculpted crater). **CIN_06**: Kael,
already there, awakens the Colossus and vanishes. Boss: *Ember Colossus*. **CIN_07**: the Ember Wind;
Kaira hears Ilio's voice.

### Ch. III — The Memory of Tides (Hydro · Nerys)
Ysmère Lagoon (sculpted bay, Archives island, flagstone causeway), Sael, three Memory Stones (the
Oath, Kael and Aelith, the Farewell). **CIN_08**: the Book of Tides — Aelith and Kael as children;
Aelis gets his name back. Boss: *Basin Sentinel*. **CIN_09**: the Tide Wind.

### Ch. IV — The Storm Oath (Electro · Torvan)
The Citadel of the Order of the Vigil on the Valdrak plateau, Orrin, three torches. Torvan confesses
he knew the Guardians were prisoners. **CIN_10**: Kael bars the door, Torvan stands in the way. Boss: *Storm Colossus*.
**CIN_11**: the four fragments unite and point to the center of the island.

### Ch. V — The Pale Lands
A bleached northern shore. Kaira finds Ilio, keeper of a lantern left by "the sad gentleman"
(Kael feeds the child). **CIN_12**. Kael's journal (five centuries). Boss: *Echo of a lost world*. Ilio joins the camp.

### Ch. VI — Aelith's Memory
The fragments on the Altar (**CIN_13**): the Sanctuary of the Four Winds, a celestial platform. Cendrel, Ysmé and Valdrak
speak of the **Third Way** (four winds, four living hearts, no prison). Boss: *Aelith's Shadow*
(a duelist). **CIN_14**: back at dawn.

### Ch. VII — The Four Vigils
Three vigil circles (ember, tide, storm); each companion faces their fear and awakens their wind
(+20% HP, +25% ATK). Minor bosses: the *Vigilants*.

### Ch. VIII — The Seal Tower
A 45 m tower at the center of the island, spiral ramp, circular summit. **CIN_15**: Kael and Aelis
explain themselves. Boss: *Kael* (duelist, mocap). **CIN_16**: he kneels, refuses to follow them
"not yet" and disappears; the white wall of the Pallor crosses the dome.

### Ch. IX — The Vigil of the Last Day
Three waves against the camp ("white tide" zone), four braziers (farewells from Kaira, Nerys, Torvan, Elara),
Bram gives his lantern, Ilio a lucky stone. **CIN_17**: night vigil, Kael sits at the fire, dawn breaks.

### Ch. X — The Last World
Elara's blessing, the top of the Tower. **CIN_18**: the Pallor takes on a face. Boss: *The Effacement*. Then the Heart of
the Seal offers three choices (choice dialogue):
1. **Restore the Seal** (CIN_19) — Aelis sacrifices himself; Aerendal survives, Kael forgets nothing.
2. **Let the world fade** (CIN_20) — a gentle ending, together.
3. **The Third Way** (CIN_21) — each carries a wind; the Pallor retreats; Aelith becomes Aelis again, Kael finally grows old.
Each ending closes with credits (subtitles).
